Educational Tools

Discover interactive platforms that help make teaching and learning more engaging, creative, and collaborative.

Mentimeter

Engage students with real-time polls, quizzes, and word clouds. Students can submit questions and respond using a code, link, or QR code, while teachers view instant results through charts and graphs.

Explore Mentimeter →

Wooclap

Make lessons interactive with matching activities, fill-in-the-blanks, image labeling, brainstorming, and collaborative message walls. It also supports leaderboards, attendance tracking, and LMS integration.

Explore Wooclap →

Slido

Encourage participation through live Q&A sessions, polls, surveys, and quizzes. Students can upvote questions and join discussions, with integrations for PowerPoint, Zoom, Google Slides, and Teams.

Explore Slido →

Genially

Create interactive presentations, infographics, and educational games using animations, clickable elements, quizzes, escape rooms, and storytelling. Use templates to build and share engaging lessons.

Explore Genially →

Blooket

Turn classroom reviews into exciting game-based learning. Choose from different game modes, use existing question sets or create your own, and motivate students through points and competition. Use live classroom games or assign homework.

Explore Blooket →
