Slido

Slido is an interactive presentation tool that allows presenters to engage their audience through live polls, question-and-answer sessions, quizzes, and surveys. It helps transform traditional presentations into interactive experiences where participants can actively share their opinions and ask questions in real time.


Top Features: Live Q&A sessions (audience can upvote questions), Polls, surveys, and quizzes, Strong integrations (PowerPoint, Zoom, Google Slides, Teams), “Rooms” feature for group discussions, Participant preview mode

Introduction

Slido is one of the most popular industry-standard tools for interaction. It is a cloud-based Q&A and polling platform designed to bridge the gap between a speaker and their audience, whether in a physical classroom or in a virtual meeting.
